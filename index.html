What this hub does
Live market intelligence and AI modules for expansion decisions
Strategic Context
Coffee & Roast Co. enters the EU market at a moment of structural opportunity. Third-wave coffee culture is still maturing across Central and Western Europe, and brand narrative — origin story, craft, sourcing transparency — is increasingly the primary purchase driver among 25–40 year old urban consumers. An Istanbul-rooted brand with genuine heritage has a differentiation advantage. This hub combines live economic data, real competition maps, and AI-driven personalization to compress 12–18 months of market research into weeks of iterative intelligence.
Market Intelligence
Economic fundamentals and competitive context
Four city scorecards benchmark each market across Brand Fit (third-wave culture, lifestyle alignment), Market Size (addressable premium consumer base), and Competition (café saturation relative to opportunity). Click any city card to load an AI-generated market narrative — a fresh strategic read on that city's dynamics, written in the context of Coffee & Roast Co.'s brand positioning.
Scoring Basis
Base scores (0–100) derived from SCA and Euromonitor-aligned market research. Static baselines; AI adds interpretive narrative per city.
Live API Data
GDP per capita and household consumption pulled directly from the World Bank Open Data API — the two best public proxies for premium purchasing power.
How To Read It
High GDP + high household spend = strong premium market. A lower Competition score means less saturation — more room for a new entrant to own a position.

City Entry Recommender
Weighted scoring model + live market fundamentals
A transparent weighted scoring model — not a black-box AI — ranks all four cities against the priorities you set. Adjust three sliders, pick an expansion mode, and see the ranking recalculate instantly. Below the tool, live Eurostat market data and an interactive café density map give structural context for each city's readiness.
The Scoring Model
Your slider weights (1–10) multiply each city's base scores for Brand Fit, Market Size, and Low Competition. The city with the highest weighted total wins — you can see exactly why.
Eurostat Data
Household food & beverage spend and urbanization rates from Eurostat. High urban population + high food spend = a market primed for specialty coffee.
The Density Map

Customer AI
Answer a few questions — AI builds your coffee persona and recommends the right Coffee & Roast Co. product
Four questions. One named coffee persona. The AI doesn't just categorize you — it writes a short editorial story about how you drink coffee, then surfaces the most relevant Coffee & Roast Co. product from the catalog. Every result is on-brand, personalized, and shareable.
How The AI Works
Answers feed a structured Claude prompt requesting a persona name, archetype summary, and 200-word customer story. A JS fallback generates one of 10 curated archetypes if the API is unavailable — this feature never fails.
Product Matching
10 catalog SKUs (Yirgacheffe, Rwandan Bourbon, Yemeni Mocha, Turkish Heritage Dark, and more) each carry flavor and occasion tags. The persona archetype maps to a tag set; top-scoring products surface as recommendations.
The Pitch
Plug in the live product catalog and this becomes a real-time personalization engine — at point-of-sale kiosks, on the website, or at café events. Built to scale.

Content Localizer
Adapt Coffee & Roast Co. marketing copy for any EU or Middle East market
Paste any Coffee & Roast Co. marketing copy and receive culturally adapted outputs for Dutch, Hungarian, German, and Arabic audiences — in seconds. This is not translation. The AI preserves the emotional intent behind the copy while rewriting idioms, register, and cultural reference points for each market.
Content Formats
Four presets with dedicated prompt guidance: Instagram Story (punchy hooks), Instagram Post (caption + hashtags), Promotional (urgency + CTA), Brand Statement (editorial, mission-driven).
Cultural Voice Rules
Dutch: direct, no-nonsense, sustainability-coded. Hungarian: warm, poetic, heritage-led. German: quality-focused, precise, premium. Arabic: storytelling, hospitality-inflected, sensory.
Why It Matters
Word-for-word translation kills brand voice in every market. This tool keeps the feeling — the reason the copy works — intact across languages without a local copywriter on retainer.
